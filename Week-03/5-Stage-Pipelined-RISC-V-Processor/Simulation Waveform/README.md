# Simulation Waveforms

## Overview

This folder contains the **RTL simulation waveforms** of the 5-Stage Pipelined RISC-V Processor, generated using **Xilinx Vivado**.

The waveforms are used to verify the behavior of the processor during simulation and to observe the movement of instructions and data through the different pipeline stages.

## Included Waveforms

The waveform results may be organized according to the major verification stages of the processor.

### 1. Processor Simulation Waveform in Compressed form:

There are three sections. Shows the overall simulation behavior of the pipelined processor, including the major pipeline signals and processor outputs.

### 2.  Processor Simulation Waveform in Expended form:

There are three sections. Shows the overall simulation behavior of the pipelined processor, including the major pipeline signals and processor outputs.


## Waveforms showing the behavior of:

  IF → ID → EX → MEM → WB
